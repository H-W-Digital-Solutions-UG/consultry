1. Executive Summary
This version keeps the final pricing structure and fixes the visualization issue: loss zones are now colored red, and margin
scaling is separated from absolute profit divergence. Absolute profit diverges after break-even, while margin naturally
approaches an upper bound because margin is a ratio. The shared H200 scenario breaks even at about 153 seats with 20%
Developer Seat attach. Margin reaches 21.6% at 200 seats, 42.5% at 300 seats, and 56.6% at 500 seats in the planning model.
The corrected margin chart removes the misleading effect of very large early negative percentages. It shows
post-break-even margin on a bounded axis and absolute profit on a separate axis. This makes the intended economics
clearer: profit diverges, margin asymptotes.

8. Datenschutz, AVV und Appliance Approach
Trust is solved by architecture plus contract, not by MIG. For DACH customers, the strongest model is a Consultry Appliance
inside the customer boundary: local connectors, local indexes, local audit, local sandboxes, local PII-gating and optional
local inference. The public no-base-fee pricing remains simple; appliance setup, managed ops and BYOI are separate
packages.

PII-gated inference and encrypted observability
                          Step                                                             Purpose

        1. Local PII detector             Regex/checksum + small local NER/PII model inside appliance

        2. Policy gate                    Allow, pseudonymize, local-only, quarantine or deny by data class

        3. Pseudonymization               Replace names/emails/customer persons with stable role-aware placeholders

        4. Hosted inference               Only pseudonymized/minimized prompt is sent when policy allows it

        5. Observability                  No raw prompt/output logs; store hashes, labels, token counts, latency, source hashes and policy decisions

        6. Customer decrypt               Sensitive payloads only customer-decryptable, e.g. passkey-gated support/observability views

Important legal nuance: not logging raw prompts reduces risk, but hosted inference still processes personal data at runtime. For Consultry-hosted
PII inference, use AVV/DPA, documented purpose limitation, no-training, no-raw-log and subprocessor transparency. For maximum data
sovereignty, run inference inside the customer appliance or BYOI environment.
