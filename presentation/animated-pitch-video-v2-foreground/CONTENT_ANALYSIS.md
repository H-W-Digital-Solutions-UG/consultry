## Narrative Arc

1. Hook: clients expect faster, more precise, more grounded consulting.
2. Problem: knowledge is fragmented across Excel, contracts, project context, heads, PCs, and tools.
3. Answer: Consultry as the system that knows the business, brand, knowledge, and projects.
4. Signal and team: detects opportunities, market trends, tenders, utilization, billing risks, and work synergies.
5. Sales and offers: turns consulting capability into the right offers for the right customers.
6. Knowledge and risk: the right answer is available when it matters, with risks visible.
7. Positioning and business case: not a generic AI chatbot, but an operating system for consulting work; one saved consulting day creates ROI.
8. CTA: fewer tools, more efficiency, more consulting, waitlist invitation.
