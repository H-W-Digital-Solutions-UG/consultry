# Consultry Second Brain Storyboard

## SB-01 — Scattered Knowledge
**Time:** 0.0-4.0s  
**Message:** Teams already know a lot, but the knowledge is spread across tools and people.  
**Visual:** Large headline with floating source cards: M365, Jira, GitHub/GitLab, SQL/NoSQL and calls.

## SB-02 — Trusted Corpus
**Time:** 3.4-7.5s  
**Message:** Approved sources become a source-bound corpus.  
**Visual:** Local files, tickets, GitHub, data and Drive connect into a Living Corpus through the Hermes Harness.

## SB-03 — Graph Memory
**Time:** 6.9-11.4s  
**Message:** Knowledge becomes graph memory with triplets, skills, decisions, risks and evidence.  
**Visual:** Calm graph layer with triplet labels and connected knowledge nodes.

## SB-04 — Backed Answers
**Time:** 10.8-15.1s  
**Message:** Teams can ask before calls and get answers grounded in sources.  
**Visual:** Second Brain View with what we know, who worked on it, repeat risks and citeable proof.

## SB-05 — Team Alignment
**Time:** 14.5-18.7s  
**Message:** Duplicate work, overlapping plans and hidden expertise become visible earlier.  
**Visual:** Alignment rails converge into better team alignment.

## SB-06 — Brand Close
**Time:** 18.1-22.0s  
**Message:** Know what your company already knows.  
**Visual:** Consultry logo lockup and final statement.
