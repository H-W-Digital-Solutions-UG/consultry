# Consultry Second Brain Video Design

Use the Consultry warm editorial brand system:

- Background: `#F4F0EA`
- Foreground: `#201E1B`
- Muted text: `#6B625B`
- Accent red: `#BF5347`
- Accent warm: `#E8913A`
- Accent cool: `#78B7C5`
- Accent purple: `#9B59B6`
- Display: `Archivo Black`
- Body: `Montserrat`
- Data labels: `IBM Plex Mono`

The film should feel calm, premium, source-grounded and useful. Avoid generic blue AI dashboards, neon HUDs, fake third-party logos, tiny unreadable interface text and busy UI mockups.

The narrative spine is knowledge and second brain: scattered work becomes trusted corpus, graph memory, backed answers, team alignment and calmer consulting work.
