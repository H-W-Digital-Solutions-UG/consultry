**Tone:** helpful and grounded, not hype-driven

## Single-Take Version

Your team already knows a lot. It is just spread across files, tickets, calls, and old projects. Consultry connects it into a second brain: sources, skills, decisions, risks, and evidence. Teams see what was done, what overlaps, and what to do next.

## TTS Notes

- Pronounce **Consultry** like **CON-sul-tree**.
- Keep short pauses after **a lot**, **old projects**, and **evidence**.
- Avoid an overly excited ad voice; this should feel like a calm product film.
