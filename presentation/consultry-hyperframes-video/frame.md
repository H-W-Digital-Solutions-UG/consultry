# Consultry HyperFrames Video Design

Use a clean pre-product marketing language: warm off-white canvas, deep charcoal typography, restrained Consultry gradient accents, thin source lines, and editorial grid composition.

The film should feel like a premium AI-workflow product reveal, not a generic SaaS dashboard demo. Avoid third-party logos, neon overload, blue/purple AI cliché gradients, dark sci-fi HUDs, and busy fake interfaces.

Composition rules:
- Large typography leads the frame.
- Every scene uses a strict left/right grid.
- Source cards are generic text labels only.
- UI panels are simplified product metaphors, not detailed application screenshots.
- Motion is calm, precise, and confident.
- The final message is: win the project, plan the work, and stay calm.
