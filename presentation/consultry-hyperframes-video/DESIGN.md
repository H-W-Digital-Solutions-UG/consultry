# Consultry Win Projects Video Design

Use the Consultry warm editorial brand system:

- Background: `#F4F0EA`
- Foreground: `#201E1B`
- Muted text: `#6B625B`
- Accent red: `#BF5347`
- Accent warm: `#E8913A`
- Accent cool: `#78B7C5`
- Accent purple: `#9B59B6`
- Display: `Archivo Black`
- Body: `Montserrat`
- Data labels: `IBM Plex Mono`

The film is the project-acquisition spine: tenders, Clay/Apollo-style selling signals, work history, consultant C.V.s, proof and offer planning.

Keep the execution warm, clean and brand-led. Avoid third-party logos, fake generated dashboards, tiny interface text, sci-fi HUD styling and overdecorated AI gradients. Use the supplied Consultry logo asset for the close.
