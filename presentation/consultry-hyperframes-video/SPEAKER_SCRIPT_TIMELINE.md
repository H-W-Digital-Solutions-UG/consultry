# Consultry Speaker Script With Timeline

**Use case:** AI narration for the 25-second HyperFrames pre-product video  
**Voice:** warm, calm, senior consultant energy  
**Pace:** medium, with short pauses after each sentence  
**Tone:** human, useful, not hype-driven  

## English Voiceover

| Time | Narration |
| --- | --- |
| 00:00-00:04.50 | New projects are rarely invisible. |
| 00:04.50-00:09.20 | They show up in tenders, Clay and Apollo signals, CRM notes, and work your consultants have already done. |
| 00:09.20-00:14.00 | Consultry connects demand with skills, proof, and C.V.s you can shape before the call. |
| 00:14.00-00:19.50 | So selling the project and planning the work become one calmer flow. |
| 00:19.50-00:25.00 | Win the project. Plan the work. Stay calm. |

## Single-Take Version

New projects are rarely invisible. They show up in tenders, Clay and Apollo signals, CRM notes, and work your consultants have already done. Consultry connects demand with skills, proof, and C.V.s you can shape before the call. So selling the project and planning the work become one calmer flow.

## German Single-Take Version

Neue Projekte sind selten unsichtbar. Sie zeigen sich in Ausschreibungen, Clay- und Apollo-Signalen, CRM-Notizen und in Arbeit, die eure Berater schon gemacht haben. Consultry verbindet Nachfrage mit Skills, Belegen und C.V.s, die ihr vor dem Call sauber zuschneiden könnt. So werden Projektverkauf und Arbeitsplanung zu einem ruhigeren Ablauf.

## TTS Notes

- Pronounce **Consultry** like **CON-sul-tree**.
- Use **C.V.s** instead of **CVs** when a narrator mispronounces the abbreviation.
- Keep a small pause after **invisible**, **done**, **call**, and **flow**.
