**Status:** Ratified Product-/Business-Domain Portfolio Baseline  
**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Scope owner:** [Define the canonical user-journey portfolio](wayfinder/consultry-product-platform-baseline/tickets/define-the-canonical-user-journey-portfolio.md)

## 1. Purpose and semantic boundary

This portfolio is the breadth-first navigation map between the ratified Whole-Consultancy Coverage Ledger and detailed scenario/Journey specifications.

- A `Business Coverage View` groups recurring situations and Jobs by the business progress they change.
- A `Journey Family` follows responsible user progress from a recognizable trigger to an observable completion state across one or more Coverage Views.
- A `Reference Thread` is one concrete, cross-view Scenario Thread used to challenge Journey and Cross-Cutting Product Contracts. It is not the complete Journey Family.
- `Journey Specification Depth` states what must be defined now and what remains a later canonical or Horizon obligation.

Journey Families are not modules, navigation areas, fixed roles, screens, Agents, graphs, technical workflows or one-to-one copies of Coverage Views. A Boutique and a Growing Specialist Consultancy share the same Journey Families; Responsibility Assignments, Participations, Authority, handoff shape, source landscape and role compression vary.

Every Coverage Anchor has one portfolio home. Cross-family movement is an explicit Handoff; it does not duplicate Journey ownership.

## 3. Three Representative Business Threads as Click-Dummy Anchor Journeys

| Reference Thread | Primary portfolio proof | Required terminal distinction | Current purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Opportunity-to-Project / External Commitment** | JF-1 and the transition into JF-2, including People/Capacity and operational readiness dependencies. | External acceptance is not Project activation; an accepted commitment still requires a supportable basis, Readiness and accepted Delivery Handoff. | Deep Anchor 1: commercial/entry continuity inside the systemic Click Dummy and later Technical PoC. |
| **Active Project/Delivery Blind-Spot and Rebuttal Simulation** | JF-2 with Knowledge, Commercial Change, People/Capacity, Operations or Steering branches where material. | A Rebuttal Candidate is not truth or approval; the terminal event is responsible disposition, revision/revalidation, Change, Recovery, escalation or stop. | Deep Anchor 2: active-work and agent-native Co-Work continuity inside the systemic Click Dummy and later Technical PoC. |
| **Knowledge/Reuse and Corporate Artifact Alignment** | JF-3 across artifacts created or synchronized in JF-1 and JF-2. | Accepted/exportable artifact version, Corpus Admission, Reusable Asset release and Reuse Application remain separate decisions. | Deep Anchor 3: Work-Result, alignment and compounding continuity inside the systemic Click Dummy and later Technical PoC. |

All three threads are first prototyped as deep corridors inside one navigable, non-functional **Systemic Platform Click Dummy**. They are not the app's navigation, module boundaries or complete Product Journey. The Click Dummy adds shallow but credible Whole-Product breadth, shared Business Objects, cross-view state and one progressive Human-AI Co-Work grammar so that the Consultry OS promise can be tested as a system. Their shared and anchor-specific Product Contracts are then consolidated to `Definition-complete` depth for a joint Three-Slice Technical PoC Handoff. Technical implementation may be staged, but no single Anchor replaces the three-anchor PoC portfolio. Full-Backend-MVP depth is selected only after PoC learning.

## 4. Horizontal experience and interaction patterns

`Personal Daily Attention and Capture` is a horizontal Experience/Continuity Journey across all families, not a seventh peer Business Journey Family. It covers My Work, Why Now, intentional Quick Capture, private/shared disposition, Context Carry-forward, defer/delegate/escalate/complete and return to responsible progress.

The horizontal entry and continuity spine is available through `My Work / Why now`, intentional Quick Capture, Search/Ask, Notification/Deep Link and direct Client-, Project-, Opportunity-, Artifact- or other work-object contexts. These entries do not force every Journey to begin with Capture or My Work.

Journey steps are translated into interaction through seven composable UX modes:

1. `Attend & Orient`
2. `Explore & Frame`
3. `Analyze & Develop`
4. `Produce & Refine`
5. `Review & Commit`
6. `Coordinate & Deliver`
7. `Observe & Adapt`

The modes are neither Journey Families, modules, screens nor a mandatory sequence. A Journey may use a subset, repeat a mode, branch, loop back or reopen after new evidence. A screen may combine adjacent modes when the user's Job, current decision and resulting Effect remain clear.

An internal library of consulting-work primitives covers attention, capture, search/retrieval, expert access, framing, investigation, analysis/diagnosis, synthesis/options, creation/adaptation, challenge/review, alignment/co-creation, decision/acceptance, coordination/handoff, execution/externalization, outcome observation, recovery and learning/reuse. It is a coverage and design checklist, not a user-facing taxonomy.

Context/Source/Rights, Responsibility/Authority/Effect, Human-AI legibility and control, Continuity/Outcome and Corporate Alignment remain cross-cutting Product contracts. Search, expert access, collaboration and AI support stay available inside the current work instead of becoming mandatory detours into separate modules.

The guided, object-/work-centered Consultry App remains the default experience. The optional Harness is another client over the same Case, Context, Source, Authority, Validation, Disposition and Outcome semantics; it does not create a separate Journey Portfolio.

### 4.1 Systemic Click-Dummy experience contract

The first clickable Prototype uses `thin platform breadth + deep anchor journeys`:

- a persistent Product shell and enough adjacent Client, Project, Knowledge, People, Commercial and Operations context to establish one Consulting-OS mental model;
- shared deterministic state so work, decisions, Artifacts/Plans, Outcomes and handoffs remain consistent across projections;
- progressive disclosure through `Attention → Work Brief → Co-Work Detail → Assurance`, without making this a mandatory step sequence;
- agent-native Co-Work bound to a responsible Job, Goal, editable Plan, Work Result/Artifact/Plan and applicable Outcome Tests;
- dynamic, allowlisted Human-Agent explanation and elicitation through context-appropriate diagrams/matrices/charts, adaptive questions with free-answer paths and editable Few-shot Suggestions;
- Chat only as a contextual control surface, with an optional Harness over the same work state.

The detailed contract is [Consultry Systemic Platform Click Dummy Experience Contract v0.1](Consultry-Systemic-Platform-Click-Dummy-Experience-Contract-v0.1.md). It ratifies the prototype direction, not final IA, screen choreography, Agent topology or full Product depth.

Research and the full semantic separation are documented in [Consulting Work Primitives and Composable UX Grammar](research/consulting-work-primitives-and-ux-grammar-2026-08.md) and ratified in [Ratify the consulting-work primitive and composable UX grammar](wayfinder/consultry-product-platform-baseline/tickets/ratify-the-consulting-work-primitive-and-composable-ux-grammar.md).

## 5. Branches and follow-up placement

- Tender Intake and Tender-to-Concept are JF-1 intake/work branches, not peer Journey Families.
- Existing-Client Project Sensing is a cross-family intake/routing branch that may create Commercial, Delivery/Change, Knowledge, People/Capacity or No-Action Cases; it creates no automatic Opportunity.
- External Client Participation is a Case Participation branch for input, clarification, review, acceptance, feedback or externalization and creates no generic Client Portal assumption.
- Corporate Artifact Alignment is a Cross-Cutting Product Contract concretely tested in the third Reference Thread.
- Governed Reuse later separates at least `Reusable Asset Release` from `Reuse Application / Service Productization` if one HITL decision cannot contain both.
- Project/Deliverable Acceptance, Closure and Operations/Learning handoffs remain explicit JF-2 obligations even when their full later Journey detail is deferred.
- Direct Expert Access, applicability conflict and unresolved expert routing remain explicit JF-3 obligations even when Search/Ask is not a standalone Journey.

## 6. Specification depth classes

### `Reference now`

A concrete Scenario Thread for both archetypes with Trigger/Preconditions, real Job and Comparator, Responsibilities/Participations/Authority candidates, nominal and rejected/off-nominal/recovery paths, relevant Objects/provisional States, Source/Record Authority, Human-AI contribution, Handoffs, terminal outcome and Acceptance Intent. It is sufficient to challenge shared contracts but not to define the entire family.

### `Canonical later`

A full Journey specification required before the relevant external pilot, recurring-use validation, mutable tenant operation or final Whole-Product Handoff. Current candidates include Personal Daily Attention/Capture, broader Project Closure, Knowledge/Expert Access, governed Reuse/Application/Productization and Adoption/Evolution.

### `Horizon contract`

A visible in-scope obligation with Job/Outcome, boundary, anchor, evidence status and Horizon intent, but no premature full step/state specification. JF-4, JF-5 and JF-6 remain here until priority, risk, prototype learning or Product Horizon selection justifies graduation.

## 7. Technical-path boundary

- `Systemic Platform Click Dummy with three deep Anchor Journeys -> Mock validation and Product Gap Register`
- `Definition-complete Three-Slice Technical PoC Handoff -> bounded Technical Wayfinder and PoC`
- `Technical PoC learning -> separate Full-Backend-MVP business and architecture decision`
- `Final Product/Business-Domain Handoff -> Whole-Product architecture and implementation planning`

The bounded Technical Validation path may therefore start before every later Journey is fully specified. It covers the three main Slices at deliberately bounded depth, must not silently broaden into whole-product implementation and must not treat deferred Journey Families as out of scope.

The Active-Project Deliverable/Decision-Basis case remains the strongest deep interaction anchor, but it no longer excludes the Opportunity-to-Project and Knowledge/Artifact-Alignment Slices from Mock or Technical PoC. Exact Acceptance, technical sequencing and reversible technical freedoms remain owned by the Three-Slice Handoff and later Technical Wayfinder.
