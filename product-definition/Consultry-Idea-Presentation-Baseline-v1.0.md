## Executive Recommendation

Consultry should present itself as an **opinionated AI work harness for consulting firms**.

That category is broad enough to hold the full product vision, but concrete enough to avoid sounding like a generic CRM, PSA, agent platform, or proposal writer. The start wedge should be named **Opportunity-to-Concept** because it cleanly covers both entry doors: a public tender and an existing-client opportunity signal.

Use this four-layer architecture everywhere:

| Layer | Canonical statement | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|
| Category | Opinionated AI work harness for consulting firms | Strategic inference from local canon + competitor research |
| Whole concept | Pre-wired consulting data model, workflows, collaboration loops, evidence rules, and bounded AI operators | Local-canon-backed |
| Start wedge | Opportunity-to-Concept: tender or client signal -> defensible concept/proposal artifact | Local-canon-backed + naming recommendation |
| First proof slice | One real, source-grounded concept/proposal section from own corpus in 5 business days | Local-canon-backed |

The deck should lead with the whole concept and prove it through the start wedge. It should not collapse the company into "AI proposal generator" or inflate the MVP into "full operating system."

## 1. One-Sentence Whole Concept

**Recommended whole-concept line:**

> Consultry is the opinionated AI work harness for consulting firms: it gives teams the consulting-specific data model, workflows, evidence rules, collaboration loops, and human-approved AI operators they would otherwise have to design themselves.

**Investor crisp:**

> Consultry gives consulting firms a pre-wired AI work harness, not CRM building blocks: the data model, workflows, evidence layer, and human-approved AI operators for winning and running consulting work.

**Customer direct:**

> Consultry connects your documents, contracts, profiles, project history, and approvals into one guided work layer for winning more consulting work and working AI-natively.

**Internal precise:**

> Consultry is a consulting-specific work harness whose product value comes from encoded consulting defaults, source-bound work artifacts, bounded operators, and human approval over a shared Win + Work corpus.

**Why this wins:** It keeps the full product visible while explaining the real differentiation against horizontal systems: consulting defaults and evidence rules, not generic storage or generic AI chat.

## 4. Why Generic AI CRMs Still Fail

**Baseline answer:**

> Flexible AI CRMs can model almost anything, but consulting firms still have to design the right objects, stages, handoffs, evidence rules, approval logic, and AI boundaries. Consultry ships those defaults for consulting work.

This should be framed as a setup-burden and domain-defaults argument, not an attack on Attio, HubSpot, or Salesforce.

| Generic AI CRM strength | Remaining gap for a consulting firm | Consultry implication |
|---|---|---|
| Custom objects and flexible data model | Customer must know which consulting objects matter: tender, award criterion, evidence pack, concept section, TeamShape, consultant profile, source binding | Ship a consulting ontology by default |
| Workflow automation | Customer must decide stages, handoffs, review gates, and risk rules | Ship Opportunity-to-Concept and Work loops |
| AI agents over CRM context | Customer must define which AI actions are allowed and how facts are sourced | Use bounded operators and source-binding |
| Enrichment and activity capture | Consulting-specific evidence, tender criteria, and proposal sections are not the native unit | Make evidence and artifacts first-class |
| Developer/API/MCP extensibility | Requires process and CRM expertise to configure well | First value should not require CRM/process design |

**Do not say:** "CRMs cannot do this."  
**Say:** "CRMs provide building blocks; Consultry provides consulting defaults."

## 7. Start Wedge: Opportunity-to-Concept

**Definition:**

Opportunity-to-Concept is the first wedge inside the larger work harness. It turns a tender or existing-client signal into a concept/proposal artifact that a consultant can review, edit, and use.

**Why this is the right wedge:**

| Reason | Explanation | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|
| High pain | Bid/concept work is non-billable, time-consuming, and quality-sensitive | Local-canon-backed + external RFP sources |
| Trust is testable | A source trail makes quality and risk visible in a short pilot | Local-canon-backed |
| It reuses the future foundation | Korpus, profiles, evidence, approvals, and audit are all useful beyond proposals | Local-canon-backed |
| It does not trap the company | The same objects expand into Work: profiles, work capture, project status, knowledge reuse | Local-canon-backed |

**Important distinction:**

> The Concept Suite is the first proof surface. Opportunity-to-Concept is the first product wedge. The AI work harness is the whole product.

## 10. Concept Suite Working Objects

The Concept Suite should be specified around working objects, not generic "AI writing."

| Object | Job | MVP bar |
|---|---|---|
| Opportunity | Holds source, owner, stage, and scope | Both tender and client signal create this |
| Evidence Pack | Collects internal and external sources that may support the artifact | Every included source has type, freshness, and relevance |
| Criteria / Need Map | Converts tender award criteria or client need into an evaluation map | Criteria are visible and weightable where available |
| Concept Plan | Structured plan for sections, proof points, risks, and team shape | Human can edit before drafting |
| Draft Section | One source-grounded section, not necessarily full proposal | Every factual claim has provenance or is blocked |
| TeamShape | Anonymous role/skill/seniority mix | No named staffing in MVP |
| Review Issue | Unsupported claim, stale source, source conflict, missing firm fact, human decision needed | Issues block approval if severe |
| Approval Card | Human responsibility and audit record | Named approver, model/prompt/source trail |

**Spec implication:** The next Concept Suite spec should define object schemas and state transitions for these objects before UI copy or generation prompts.

## 11. Evidence/Trust Model

Trust is the product spine.

| Rule | Product behavior | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|
| Firm facts need tenant-corpus support | A claim about capabilities, references, certifications, team skills, or track record cannot persist without a source | Local-canon-backed |
| External facts need citeable external sources | Regulation, standards, market facts, or public claims require ExternalSource and freshness | Local-canon-backed |
| Model expertise must be marked | Methodology, wording, framing, and reasoning can come from the model but must not masquerade as fact | Local-canon-backed |
| Human approval is the legal/operational backstop | AI can assist; a named human approves and remains responsible | Local-canon-backed |
| Faithfulness is checked before approval | The source must support the claim; unsupported claims become review issues | Local-canon-backed |
| Audit is append-only | Operator call, model version, prompt version, source bindings, approval, and state change are logged | Local-canon-backed |
| No autonomous submit | Consultry produces internal artifacts and export, not external submission in MVP | Local-canon-backed |

**Customer proof line:**

> Every factual claim is either sourced, flagged, or removed before a human approves the artifact.

**Risk note:** Do not claim "100% grounded." Say "source-bound, checked, and human-approved."

## 15. PMF Proof

**Recommended PMF event:**

> A design partner uses Consultry to create one real, source-grounded concept/proposal section from its own corpus within 5 business days, trusts the evidence trail enough to use it in internal bid preparation, edits inside Consultry, and does not restart from scratch in Word.

**Success metrics:**

| Metric | Success threshold | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Time to first usable section | <= 5 business days from corpus handoff | Matches local PMF bar |
| Own-corpus use | At least 10 relevant internal assets or one real tender/client file plus supporting references | Prevents generic ChatGPT demo |
| Source coverage | 100% of firm/external factual claims have source binding or are blocked | Trust spine |
| Edit behavior | User edits the draft in Consultry before export | Shows it is a working artifact |
| Restart behavior | User does not rewrite from blank Word doc | Anti-success guard |
| Review issue value | At least one unsupported/stale/weak claim is caught or explicitly approved | Shows evidence layer matters |
| Internal use | Artifact is shared in real internal bid/concept preparation | "Real used" definition |
| Qualitative trust | Primary user says evidence trail is good enough for internal preparation | Human acceptance |

**Kill or pivot signals:**

| Signal | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| User copies text to Word and rewrites from scratch | Artifact quality or workflow trust failed |
| Evidence pack is ignored | Trust model is not delivering perceived value |
| User treats it as generic ChatGPT | Category and workflow are too weak |
| Setup requires a process/CRM expert | "Pre-wired" promise failed |
| Unsupported firm claims pass into accepted draft | Product risk, not messaging issue |
| Only partner uses it, consultants do not edit | Seat-utilization risk |

**Design-partner test script:**

1. Select one live or recently real tender/client opportunity.
2. Load minimum corpus: past proposals, references, capability docs, contracts where relevant, certificates, and one methodology asset.
3. Build Evidence Pack and Criteria/Need Map with user review.
4. Generate Concept Plan before any prose.
5. Draft exactly one high-value section.
6. Review unsupported claims and source trail with the user.
7. Ask the user to edit in Consultry and export.
8. Debrief: trust, missing sources, time saved, whether they would use it in the next real pursuit, and what would make them very disappointed to lose it.

## 18. Sales Script Baseline

### Bestandskunden-Led Script

> Most mid-small consultancies do not lack a CRM dashboard; they lack a reliable way to turn existing-client signals into next work. We start from contracts and firm documents, identify a real opportunity, build the evidence pack, and produce a concept section your team can review with sources visible.

**Discovery questions:**

1. Which existing-client renewals, options, or follow-on signals do you currently miss or find late?
2. Where do contracts and account knowledge live today?
3. When a partner says "we should propose the next phase," who turns that into a concept and how long does it take?
4. Would a source-grounded first section in 5 days be useful enough to test on a real account?

### Tender-Led Script

> For public-sector-heavy firms, the bottleneck is not seeing a tender; it is qualifying it honestly and producing a defensible answer without burning days. Consultry parses criteria, checks evidence against your corpus, builds a concept plan, and drafts a source-grounded section for human approval.

**Discovery questions:**

1. How many tenders do you reject because bid effort is too high?
2. What facts are legally or reputationally dangerous if the AI invents them?
3. Where do references, certificates, capability statements, and past answers live?
4. What would make a draft trustworthy enough for internal bid preparation?

### Investor Script

> Attio and Salesforce give companies flexible AI CRM building blocks. Loopio and AutogenAI help with response production. Consultry is different: it starts with a painful consulting workflow - Opportunity-to-Concept - and uses that proof to build the broader AI work harness for consulting firms, with consulting ontology, evidence rules, bounded AI operators, and DACH compliance posture built in.

**We win if:** a consulting firm says, "We could technically configure this in a CRM, but Consultry already knows our work, evidence, and approval rules."

**We lose if:** users treat Consultry as another generic AI writer, a tender feed, or a CRM that still needs a process expert.

**Product implications:**

1. Make Evidence Pack and Review Issues visible in the demo.
2. Never demo only generated prose.
3. Preserve both entry doors in every narrative.
4. Keep setup lightweight enough that "pre-wired" is believable.
5. Make consultant editing behavior a first-class metric.

## Final Baseline Summary

Use this summary when starting any deck, website, or product spec:

> Consultry is the opinionated AI work harness for consulting firms. It ships the consulting-specific data model, workflows, evidence rules, collaboration loops, and human-approved AI operators that firms would otherwise have to design themselves. We prove the harness through Opportunity-to-Concept: a tender or existing-client signal becomes an evidence pack, criteria/need map, concept plan, grounded draft section, review issues, and human-approved export. The first PMF proof is one real source-grounded concept/proposal section from a design partner's own corpus in five business days. From there, the same harness expands from Win into Work: profiles, work capture, project status, knowledge reuse, approvals, and audit.
