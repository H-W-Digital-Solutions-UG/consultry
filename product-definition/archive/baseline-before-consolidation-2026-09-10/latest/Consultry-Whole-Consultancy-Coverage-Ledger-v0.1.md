# Consultry — Whole-Consultancy Coverage Ledger v0.1

**Status:** Ratified Product-/Business-Domain Coverage Baseline  
**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Scope owner:** [Define Consultancy Jobs and Responsibility Coverage](wayfinder/consultry-product-platform-baseline/tickets/define-canonical-responsibility-and-job-families.md)

## 1. Purpose and boundary

This ledger is the breadth-first coverage check for the long-term Consultry Product Scope. It prevents important consultancy work from disappearing before Reference Threads, Journeys, Product Behaviors, Requirements and Horizons are derived.

The coverage structure is neither a module catalog nor an organizational, role, permission, workspace, bounded-context or technical architecture. It is deliberately not a strict MECE partition: real consultancy work crosses views. Each Anchor Scope Trace has one primary view based on the business progress it changes and names every material cross-view handoff instead of duplicating the trace.

`Coverage-complete` means that no material Product-Vision area silently disappears. It does not prove priority, Consultry causality, willingness to pay, adoption, retention or the depth required in the first rollout horizon.

## 2. Ratified coverage structure

### 2.1 Six business coverage views

| Business Coverage View | Recognizable trigger and progress | Primary completion state | Important boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Win & Commit Client Work** | A client/account signal, tender, expressed need or growth situation must be qualified, shaped, commercially framed and either rejected or responsibly committed. | Rejected/no-bid with rationale, or an externally accepted and internally supportable commitment ready for activation. | Owns external commercial commitment; Delivery owns engagement readiness and outcome after the handoff. |
| **Deliver & Assure Client Outcomes** | Accepted work must be activated, performed, challenged, reviewed, adapted, recovered and accepted against the engagement basis. | Accepted client/engagement outcome, responsible closure or explicit abort/recovery state. | Includes personal/expert work and artifact production inside delivery; does not own governed reusable-asset truth. |
| **Use, Learn & Reuse Expertise** | A person needs applicable firm/expert knowledge, or work creates learning that may become a reusable method, asset, skill or offer component. | Source-visible application, responsibly accepted learning, or governed reusable asset/application with measured outcome. | Client-specific artifact acceptance remains with its originating work; corpus admission and reuse release are separate decisions. |
| **Mobilize People & Develop Capability** | Committed or expected demand exposes a staffing, capacity, experience or capability gap. | Responsible allocate/reassign/develop/build/buy/partner/hire/defer/decline decision. | Owns people/capability response; Delivery owns whether an engagement can responsibly proceed. |
| **Operate the Consultancy** | Work, commitments or operational events require complete time, expense, cost, vendor, license, billing, invoice or other backoffice/economic records and dispositions. | Operational or billing readiness, corrected authoritative record, approved action, or explicitly unresolved exception. | Owns operational and economic facts/readiness; it does not make portfolio priorities from them. |
| **Steer the Firm & Portfolio** | Cross-client, project, offer, capability, capacity, risk or economic evidence requires prioritization and allocation. | Continue/hold/stop/invest/reallocate/escalate decision with observable follow-up. | Owns portfolio and firm-level judgment; underlying project, people and economic facts retain their source authority. |

`Operate the Consultancy` and `Steer the Firm & Portfolio` remain distinct because the former establishes operational/economic truth and readiness while the latter makes allocative and strategic decisions from that evidence.

### 2.2 Separate enabling lane

| Enabling lane | Purpose | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **Adopt & Evolve Consultry** | Bring a Target Consultancy from source/corpus/context setup to its first accepted Value Case, then govern changes to applicable policies, methods, templates, brand rules, skills, processes and configuration. | Mandatory Product Lifecycle, but not a seventh peer business-value view or an independent proof of Target-Consultancy value. Project/scope changes remain in Delivery and, where contractual, Commercial. |

### 2.3 Cross-cutting trace dimensions, not additional views

- Personal/Expert Work and Work Artifacts;
- Knowledge-to-Action, Blind-Spot Coverage and Rebuttal Simulation;
- Corporate Artifact Alignment across Knowledge, Brand/CD and Governance/Release;
- Responsibility, Case Participation, Authority, delegation and actual-person separation of duties;
- Source/Record Authority, Evidence, Counterevidence, Rights, Provenance and uncertainty;
- Human-AI responsibility, validation, disposition and controlled external effects;
- Context, decision, handoff, recovery, outcome and learning continuity;
- external-client or reviewer participation;
- Boutique role compression versus distributed Growing-Consultancy handoffs.

App, Harness, Agent, Skill, Graph, Model Bridge, integration, workspace and module are possible later realization or packaging forms and never create a Coverage View by themselves.

### 3.2 Boundaries, archetypes, outcomes and evidence

`RC` means a role-compressed Boutique may assign several responsibilities to the same Person; the trace must show responsibility-context switches and actual-person conflicts rather than inventing organizational handoffs. `DIST` means a Growing Specialist Consultancy will more often require explicit cross-role/team/system handoff and acceptance. Both variations apply to every Anchor unless a later journey proves otherwise.

## 4. Coverage-complete gate

The breadth-first gate is satisfied because:

1. every Product-Vision area is represented by at least one Anchor Scope Trace;
2. client acquisition/commitment, recurring expert/delivery work, compounding, people/capability, operations/economics, firm steering and product adoption are present;
3. every Anchor identifies a recurring Job, accountable Responsibility, decision/handoff, business object, blind spot and implementation-independent Required Product Behavior;
4. every Anchor is stress-tested for role-compressed and distributed operation without turning job titles into technical roles;
5. cross-cutting Corporate Artifact Alignment, knowledge, source, authority, rights, Human-AI, recovery and external-effect obligations remain visible without becoming duplicate views;
6. evidence status and a provisional Acceptance Intent remain explicit;
7. detailed states, Business Rules, quantitative thresholds, Journey depth, feature realization, rollout horizon and technical architecture remain assigned to later decisions.

## 5. Explicitly not decided here

- the number or names of product modules, workspaces, apps or navigation areas;
- fixed roles, departments, permissions or technical principals;
- detailed Journey steps, universal states or Business Object lifecycles;
- which Coverage View supplies the first Validation/MVP Slice;
- quantitative Acceptance thresholds or Commercial packaging;
- physical graph, agent, storage, Model Bridge, Harness or integration architecture.
